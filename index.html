About

I am a postdoctoral fellow at the Robotics and Internet of Things Unit (RIOTU), Prince Sultan University. My work sits at the intersection of electrical engineering, embedded computer science, robotics, artificial intelligence, and sustainable energy systems.

With over 8 years of university teaching experience and expertise in advanced technology, I focus on developing intelligent autonomous systems for real-world challenges including UAV navigation in GPS-denied environments, battery health monitoring for electric vehicles, and IoT/AI integration for smart systems.

My research has resulted in 15+ Q1 journal publications, 11+ conference papers, and multiple research grant proposals. I am passionate about bridging theory and practice, mentoring students, and creating solutions that advance Saudi Vision 2030 through innovation and sustainable technology.

Research

My research focuses on autonomous UAV navigation, embedded AI, robotics, battery health monitoring, and intelligent energy systems.

🛩️ Autonomous UAV Localization

Non-visual and terrain-aided navigation using sensor fusion, Kalman filtering, and deep learning for robust positioning in GNSS-denied environments.

📊 Open-Source Dataset Generation

Creating reproducible UAV datasets using ROS 2, Gazebo, and PX4 with multi-sensor data for community-driven research.

🔋 Energy Storage Systems

Hybrid Li-ion and supercapacitor systems, state-of-health monitoring, and power management for electric vehicles.

⚡ Power Electronics

DC/DC converter design, MPPT strategies, renewable energy integration, photovoltaic and wind systems.

🌐 Smart Sensing & IoT

Embedded sensing, wearable health monitoring, IoT architectures, intelligent automation systems.

🧠 Machine Learning & AI

CNN-LSTM architectures, Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks, and hybrid models for real-world deployment.
